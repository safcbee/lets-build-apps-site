Five calm questions. One practical setup.

No meter reading or camera-menu fluency required. Choose the closest answer and Better Pics explains every recommendation in plain English.

01 Describe the picture

Choose portrait, family, pets, sport, birds, landscape, macro or night, then describe the subject’s movement.

02 Name the lens and light

Select the mounted RF lens, its current focal length and the light you can see. “Not sure” is a supported answer.

03 Set up and shoot

Get exposure mode, shutter, aperture, ISO, autofocus, drive and stabilisation guidance, plus what to change if frame one misses.

Review the shot while it still matters

Facts first. Suggestions second.

Choose an image from Photos or Files. Better Pics reads the settings the file actually contains and analyses the rendered picture privately on the iPhone.

EXIF See the recorded setup

Camera, lens, shutter speed, aperture, ISO and focal length appear when the file includes them. Missing settings are never invented.

LIGHT Notice tonal risks

On-device analysis looks at luminance, deep shadows and near-white highlights to suggest a measured exposure change when useful.

FRAME Inspect the next-frame clues

Visible edge definition, faces and subject placement support practical advice—without pretending a whole photograph can be reduced to a score.

Private by design: the selected original is not uploaded, copied into a Better Pics library or retained after you leave the result. Only an optional small text summary can stay on the device.

Support without the runaround

Questions before the next frame?

These answers cover the common first steps. For anything else, email support@letsbuildhq.com.

Which picture should I import for review?

Use the original camera JPEG or HEIF when possible. Better Pics can also try a RAW file that the iPhone can decode. Screenshots, messaging apps and some editing exports often remove camera settings.

Why are shutter, aperture or ISO missing?

The selected file did not include those EXIF fields. Better Pics still offers careful image-only observations and says when a conclusion is limited by missing metadata.

Does Better Pics upload or keep my photograph?

No. The chosen file is decoded temporarily in memory for on-device analysis. It is not sent to a Better Pics server, added to an app library or saved in review history.

Will the suggested setup guarantee the shot?

No camera recipe can guarantee a result. Subject behaviour, changing light, technique and creative intent still matter. Check the frame, then use the rescue guidance to make one deliberate change.

Is Better Pics an official Canon app?

No. Better Pics is independently developed by Brian Leary. Canon and EOS are Canon Inc. trademarks, and the current Canon manual remains the definitive reference.
