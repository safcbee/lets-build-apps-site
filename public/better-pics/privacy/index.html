Better Pics privacy policy.

Better Pics has no account, adverts, tracking, analytics SDK or app-operated cloud service. A photo is processed on your iPhone only after you choose it.

Last updated: 28 July 2026

The short version

Better Pics is an offline-first camera companion. Its guided setup and Canon field guide do not need an account. When you choose a photo, the app analyses it on the device. Better Pics does not upload the selected original or operate a separate photo, sync or analytics service.

Photos and metadata

When you choose a picture from Photos or Files, Better Pics temporarily decodes that file in memory to read available EXIF/TIFF camera settings and analyse the rendered pixels with Apple frameworks.

  • The selected photo is not uploaded to Better Pics or another app-operated server.
  • It is not added to a Better Pics photo library or retained after you leave the result.
  • Analysis may consider luminance, deep shadows, near-white highlights, visible edge definition, faces and subject placement.
  • Camera, lens, shutter, aperture, ISO and focal length appear only when the file contains those fields. Better Pics does not infer an absent setting.
  • GPS coordinates and camera serial number are not displayed or written to review history.

Some images—especially screenshots, messaging-app copies and edited exports—contain little or no camera metadata. A camera JPEG or HEIF usually provides the clearest review. A RAW file works only when the iPhone can decode it.

Review history

Better Pics can keep up to 20 small review summaries in its private local preferences. A summary may contain the review date, headline, camera and lens names, shutter, aperture, ISO and one suggested action.

A summary does not contain the original photo, a thumbnail, GPS coordinates or camera serial number. You can clear all review summaries at any time from the Camera tab.

Device backups

Apple may include local app preferences in an iCloud or computer backup according to the backup settings you control. Better Pics does not operate a separate sync or backup service.

Apple services

  • Photos and Files: Apple’s pickers provide access only after you choose the relevant action and select an image.
  • On-device processing: Apple ImageIO and Vision frameworks are used to decode metadata and support local image observations.
  • TestFlight or App Store diagnostics: Apple may handle diagnostics under your device and Apple privacy settings. Better Pics contains no third-party diagnostic or advertising SDK.

Your controls

You can clear review summaries inside Better Pics. Deleting the app removes its local data from the iPhone, subject to any device backups you control. Photo access can be changed in iOS Settings.

Better Pics does not sell personal information, build advertising profiles or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
