Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you begin.

Who is Let’s Build Sentences for?

It is for children beginning to recognise letter sounds, form letters and write complete sentences, with a parent or caregiver helping to choose suitable practice.

Can I create my own sentences?

Yes. Parents can create custom sentences or use one-tap child-friendly sentence generation.

Does my child have to trace every letter?

No. Letter tracing is optional, and the app also includes typing and handwriting modes.

Can letters and sentences be spoken aloud?

Children can tap letters to hear sounds. Parents can make recordings, choose a standard iOS narrator and adjust speech style.

Is there a subscription or advertising?

No. There is a free starter experience and a one-time premium purchase, with no subscription and no advertising.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Let’s Build Sentences is available for iPhone and iPad and supports iCloud sync.

Ready to build their next sentence?

Begin with the free starter experience, then unlock premium once if it feels right for your family.
