Privacy in context

Shared by design, not public by default.

Family Trips uses Sign in with Apple and an EU-hosted Supabase backend. Trip content is visible to authenticated members of the invited family space.

No advertising or tracking SDKs

The app does not use behavioural advertising or cross-app tracking.

Account controls

Account deletion is available in the app. Shared family content may remain for other members unless separately removed.
