Let’s Build Apps HQ is the home of focused, independently designed Apple apps. The collection turns specific everyday moments—from a child’s first sentences to family anticipation, travel memories and camera settings—into calm, carefully considered software.

  1. 01
    Current public position

    Four apps are available on the App Store; five further products are in review, private testing or release preparation.

  2. 02
    Commercial terms

    Models vary by app. Some use a one-time unlock; others have optional subscriptions or are currently free. Apple shows the exact local terms before purchase.

  3. 03
    Privacy

    The website has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking pixels. Each product page links to the privacy details that apply to that app.
