App support.

For help with any app from Let’s Build Apps HQ, email support@letsbuildappshq.com.

What to include

Please tell us the app name, your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS or iPadOS version, and what you expected to happen. A screenshot can help, but do not include passwords, payment details, private travel documents or other sensitive information.

Privacy and account requests

The same address handles privacy questions, data-access requests and deletion requests. The privacy page explains storage and services app by app.
