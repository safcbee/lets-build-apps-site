Focused, not fussy

The useful details, ready when you need them.

01Complete shot logging

Capture dose, yield, time, grind, equipment, taste, rating and notes.

02Explainable dial-in help

Use successful shots with the same bean to choose one controlled adjustment—not a promise of laboratory precision.

03Find and scan beans

Search selected roaster catalogues, connect compatible stores, scan a barcode or recognise label text on-device.

04Recipes and equipment

Save templates plus machine, grinder and basket profiles, then recall the best recipe for a bean.

05Back up on your terms

Export CSV or create and restore a complete library backup through Files, including iCloud Drive when you choose it.

The current app has no advertising, subscription or in-app purchase.
