Privacy Policy

Our apps are designed to work without advertising, tracking, account requirements or server-side profiles.

Effective: February 2026

Our commitment

At Let’s Build Apps, we believe your digital footprints are your own. We prioritise privacy and peace of mind across the app family.

Data collection

We do not collect, sell or track personal information, usage data or device identifiers. App data stays on your device, or in your own Apple iCloud account where an app offers sync.

Better Coffee bean scanner

When using the bean scanner in Let’s Build Better Coffee, the barcode is sent to Open Food Facts at openfoodfacts.org to retrieve product information. No personal data accompanies this request.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties. There are no tracking SDKs, advertising networks or measurement engines hidden in our code.

Family privacy

Because some apps are made for family use, they are designed to avoid behavioural tracking, advertising profiles and child-directed data collection.

  • No in-app account registration required.
  • No geolocation tracking.
  • No behavioural advertising.

Questions

For privacy questions, email privacy@leary.cloud. For general help, email support@leary.cloud.
