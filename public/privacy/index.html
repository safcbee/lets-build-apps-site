Privacy, app by app.

Our apps do not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Some features use Apple services or carefully selected service providers; the details below explain what is used and why.

Our commitment

Let’s Build Apps is developed by SAFCMedia (Brian Leary). We do not sell personal information, build advertising profiles, or track people across other companies’ apps and websites. Data is used only to provide the feature you choose to use, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations.

This policy describes the current implementations we reviewed. Apple, GitHub, Supabase, Open Food Facts and FatSecret process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

This website

This is a content-led public website hosted from GitHub. We have not added accounts, forms, cookies, advertising, analytics or behavioural tracking. If you follow an App Store link, Apple handles that visit. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include.

As with most web hosting, GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser details and timestamps to deliver and secure the site. We do not use those logs to create visitor profiles.

Let’s Build Sentences

The app does not require a Let’s Build Apps account and has no custom advertising or analytics service.

  • On-device and iCloud data: settings and learning content are stored on the device. If iCloud is available, selected settings, sentences and recordings can sync through your Apple iCloud account.
  • Voice features: microphone access is requested only when a parent chooses to record audio. Apple’s Personal Voice and speech features are handled by iOS under the permissions and settings you control.
  • Sounds and handwriting: letter taps, tracing and handwriting feedback are processed within the app.
  • Purchases: the optional one-time premium unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive payment-card details.

Let’s Build Countdowns

The app works without a Let’s Build Apps account. Moments are stored locally first.

  • iCloud: Countdowns Plus can use Apple’s private CloudKit database for sync, shared Moments, memory notes and photos. Apple controls the iCloud account and infrastructure.
  • Sharing and widgets: content is sent only when you use Apple’s Share Sheet or an app link. Widgets display data supplied by the app on your device.
  • Purchases: the one-time Countdowns Plus unlock is processed by Apple. There is no subscription and we do not receive payment-card details.

Let’s Build Family Trips

Family Trips needs an account and online service so invited family members can see the same trip. Its dedicated privacy policy contains the full details.

  • Authentication: Sign in with Apple provides a secure account identifier and, when shared, profile information. We never receive your Apple password.
  • Storage and sync: Supabase hosts account and trip data, messages, costs, itineraries, photos and documents in its EU region. Access controls restrict shared content to authenticated members of the relevant family space.
  • Notifications: Apple Push Notification service processes a device token when notifications are enabled.
  • Family sharing: information added to a shared family space is visible to its invited members. Removing an account does not automatically remove content that other family members still need in their shared space; the dedicated policies explain deletion and retention.
  • No purchases: Family Trips currently has no subscription, paywall or in-app purchase.

Let’s Build Travel Plans

The current Travel Plans V2 product is local-first and does not use the Family Trips account or Supabase backend. Its dedicated privacy policy explains the current development implementation.

  • Local data: the free trip and its planning information are stored in the app’s private Application Support area.
  • Plus iCloud features: the latest development build uses Apple CloudKit and CKShare for sync, family trip sharing, chat and document-vault assets.
  • Documents: files, photos and scans are added only when you choose them. Apple Vision and PDFKit recognise text on the device.
  • On-device assistance: supported iOS 26 devices use Apple Foundation Models locally for note import and grounded trip checks.
  • Purchases: monthly or annual Plus is processed by Apple StoreKit and is an individual entitlement, not a family-wide Supabase subscription.

Let’s Build Good Habits

Good Habits is a food and nutrition diary. It does not require an account. Profile, diary, recipe, shopping and preference information is stored in the app’s local storage.

  • HealthKit: if you grant access, the app can read steps and active energy and can write water and logged nutrition. It uses only the permissions you approve, and this information is not used for advertising.
  • Barcode scanning: camera access is used when you choose to scan a product. A barcode can be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve food information.
  • Food search: a search phrase and the region code GB are sent to the apps.leary.cloud food-search service, which relays the request to FatSecret. Do not enter personal or sensitive information into the food search box.

Good Habits provides logging and general informational tools. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a replacement for medical or personalised nutrition advice.

Let’s Build Better Coffee

Better Coffee does not require an account. Shot logs, beans, profiles, recipes and preferences are stored locally on the device.

  • Camera and text recognition: camera access is used only when you choose to scan a barcode or coffee label. Apple Vision can recognise label text on the device.
  • Product lookup: a scanned barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve matching product information.
  • No cloud account: the current app has no Let’s Build Apps account or cloud sync service.

Your choices and controls

You can manage microphone, camera, HealthKit, notifications, Personal Voice and iCloud permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete locally stored information using the relevant in-app controls or by deleting the app. Family Trips provides account deletion and export controls; Travel Plans provides trip archive, export and “Delete forever” controls.

Children and families

Our family and learning apps avoid behavioural advertising and cross-app tracking. Parents and guardians should supervise the information children create or share, especially in shared family spaces and voice recordings.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice when an app or service changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. For a privacy request, email privacy@leary.cloud. For product help, email support@leary.cloud.
