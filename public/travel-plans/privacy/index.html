Privacy Policy

How the current local-first Travel Plans 2.0 app uses your iPhone and optional iCloud Drive storage.

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Who operates the app

Let’s Build Travel Plans is developed by Let’s Build Apps HQ, referred to here as “we”, “us” or “our”. Privacy questions can be sent to support@letsbuildappshq.com.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Travel Plans 2.0 does not require an account, Sign in with Apple login or Supabase account.

Trip information stays on this device

Trips, itinerary items, packing lists, payments, document references, traveller details, emergency contacts, notes, settings and archive information are stored in the app’s private container on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData.

The app has no developer-operated account or trip database. It does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

Booking files and optional iCloud Drive

Files you deliberately attach—such as booking PDFs, tickets and photos—are stored in the app’s private Documents area. You can optionally move these booking files to the app’s private iCloud Drive container so they are available through your Apple account. This setting affects booking files only: trip facts remain stored locally on the device.

Apple operates iCloud and applies the iCloud settings, security and retention associated with your Apple account. We do not receive your Apple password or get a copy of your files on a Let’s Build Apps HQ server.

Files, photos and import limits

The app can import files or photos only when you choose those actions. Imported files are limited to 50 MB. The app may read file metadata needed to display, categorise and open the attachment, but does not upload it to us.

Maps and sharing

  • MapKit: Apple MapKit may retrieve map imagery used for destination previews. Travel Plans 2.0 does not request your current location.
  • Exports: information and booking files leave the app only when you choose an Apple share or file action and select a destination.

Recovery and deletion

If the local SwiftData store cannot be opened, Travel Plans moves the unreadable store files into a dated recovery folder before creating a fresh store. Booking files in Documents are not deleted by this recovery process.

You can archive trips and permanently delete them from inside the app. Deleting the app removes its local container. Booking files moved to iCloud Drive may remain in iCloud until you remove them through the app or your Apple iCloud storage controls.

Children and sensitive details

Travel Plans is a general travel-planning utility and is not directed to children. You control the information you enter. Avoid adding passport numbers, medical details or other sensitive information unless it is needed for your trip and you are comfortable storing it on the device or in your private iCloud Drive container.

TestFlight and release status

This policy covers Travel Plans 2.0 build 1, currently available through private TestFlight. We will update it if the release implementation or data handling changes.

Contact

Privacy and product support: support@letsbuildappshq.com.
