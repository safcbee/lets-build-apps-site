Current TestFlight build

Travel Plans Plus is built on Apple services.

Build 37 brings the current Plus implementation to TestFlight, including Apple-backed sync, sharing, chat and document storage alongside the local-first free experience.

☁CloudKit sync and sharing

Plus uses the family’s iCloud accounts for trip sharing, sync and family chat—without a Let’s Build server account.

▣Document vault

Import, photograph or scan travel files; Apple Vision recognises text on the device.

✦On-device assistance

Supported iOS 26 devices can import notes and surface grounded checks using Apple Foundation Models on-device.

♪Reminders and themes

Plus adds local reminders and a choice of system, light or dark appearance.

∞Unlimited trips

Plus removes the one-active-trip boundary and keeps templates and exports available across trips.

£Individual subscription

The current plan is £2.99 monthly or £19.99 annually. Each person who wants Plus subscribes individually through Apple.

Release note: build 37 is the current verified TestFlight upload. Its signed production CloudKit configuration matches the deployed Travel Plans schema.

Two travel apps, two clear choices

Travel Plans is not Family Trips.

Travel Plans is the local-first, five-pillar product with an optional individual Plus subscription. Family Trips is the separate, completely free shared-family app using Sign in with Apple and Supabase.
