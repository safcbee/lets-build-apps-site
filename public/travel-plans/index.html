Little details, easier days

Keep the plan flexible.

Move or copy an activity to another day with its photo. Share a selected day as images, or choose a day or the whole itinerary as a PDF. Preview the result, choose whether to include times, directions and the cover photo, then select where to send it.

Plan · Ideas · Essentials

A place for the possibilities and the practicalities.

01Days you can shape

Switch days by tapping their dates. Add places, meals, stays and travel, with a photo or map to bring each activity into view.

02Ideas worth keeping

Search Apple Maps, add a manual idea or save an HTTPS link. Choose an optional photo, then decide when the idea belongs on your plan. Manual planning works offline.

03Save a link from another app

Choose Travel Plans in the iPhone share sheet, edit the link title and choose a trip. Open Travel Plans and confirm the import into Ideas. Saving the link does not fetch or scan the page.

04The essentials, close by

Keep packing, spending, documents, travellers, notes and photos in Essentials. Track amounts and due dates in your chosen currency.

05Your choice of next step

Open a saved website, directions or an external research link only when you choose it. Check opening times, accessibility, reservations and photo rights with the source.

06A plan you can pass on

Preview a selected itinerary PDF or day images before using Apple’s share sheet. Full trip backups are a separate export and can include private documents.

Local-first by design

Your plans stay yours.

Unlimited local trips, stored privately on your iPhone. There is no Travel Plans account, advertising profile or analytics SDK. Booking files stay on your device unless you choose optional iCloud Drive storage.

Privacy at a glance

  • Trip facts stay on this device; trip sync is off
  • No account or Sign in with Apple required
  • No advertising or analytics SDK
  • Optional iCloud Drive is only for booking files
  • Sharing starts with your choice and a preview
  • Complete trip backups include attachments and restore as new copies
  • An unreadable database is preserved; the app stops rather than replacing it
