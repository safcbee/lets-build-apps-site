Local-first by design

Your plans stay yours.

Version 2.0 supports unlimited trips stored privately on your iPhone. There is no Travel Plans account, advertising profile or analytics SDK.

Privacy at a glance

  • Trip facts are stored on this device
  • No account or Sign in with Apple required
  • No advertising or analytics SDK
  • Optional iCloud Drive is only for booking files
  • Database recovery preserves files instead of deleting them

Made safer for testing

The details behind this build.

The 2.0 TestFlight build has been rebuilt around the new Let’s Build Apps HQ identity and checked as a signed, release-ready iPhone app.

↺Safer recovery

If the local trip database cannot be opened, its files are quarantined for recovery rather than silently erased.

☁Optional file continuity

Move booking files between on-device storage and your private iCloud Drive. Trip facts remain local.

◎Truthful readiness

Tickets and attached files now count toward Paperwork, and the next action reflects what is genuinely unfinished.

50Bounded imports

File imports are limited to 50 MB to protect memory and keep the app responsive.

19Automated checks

Nineteen app tests pass, alongside a clean Release analysis, signed archive and package validation.
